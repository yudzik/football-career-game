They won the [[2007 Russian Premier League|2007]], [[2010 Russian Premier League|2010]], [[2011\u201312 Russian Premier League|2011\u201312]], [[2014\u201315 Russian Premier League|2014\u201315]], [[2018\u201319 Russian Premier League|2018\u201319]], [[2019\u201320 Russian Premier League|2019\u201320]], [[2020\u201321 Russian Premier League|2020\u201321]], [[2021\u201322 Russian Premier League|2021\u201322]], [[2022\u201323 Russian Premier League|2022\u201323]], [[2023\u201324 Russian Premier League|2023\u201324]] and [[2025\u201326 Russian Premier League|2025\u201326]] seasons of the Russian Premier League, as well as the [[2007\u201308 UEFA Cup]] and the [[2008 UEFA Super Cup]]. The club is owned and sponsored by the Russian state-owned energy giant [[Gazprom]]. The team plays its home matches at the [[Krestovsky Stadium|Gazprom Arena]].
